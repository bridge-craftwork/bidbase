About

CardShark BidBase is Nelson Ford's bridge bidding program, written in Visual Basic since about 2000. Its knowledge is a database of rules: each names the auction so far, a call, and what the hand needs (points, suit lengths and strength, shape, stoppers, position, vulnerability). For a hand, BidBase tries the auction's rules in order and makes the call of the first rule the hand fits. Conventions can be switched on and off.

This page runs a Rust port of Nelson's engine, compiled to WebAssembly, on his 2026 rules (16,786) or the 2018 SourceForge release (5,465). Nothing is sent anywhere: it all runs in your browser. Where no rule fits, BidBase passes; Nelson's own practice program can also choose a call by double-dummy analysis there, which this page does not do yet.

BidBase is Nelson Ford's work. His site says: "All the databases, text files and programs making up BidBase are free public domain." The port is at github.com/bridge-craftwork/bidbase, with Nelson's programs and rules.
